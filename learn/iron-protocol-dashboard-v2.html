CHAPTER 05

SUPPLEMENT STACK

Timing is everything. Absorption windows matter.

MULTIVITAMIN
10:00 AM · with breakfast
Fat-soluble vitamins (A, D, E, K) absorb with the dietary fat from paneer and ghee. Don't take on an empty stomach.
OMEGA-3
SPLIT · 1g lunch · 1-2g dinner
2-3g EPA/DHA total. Always with fat-containing meals. Reduces joint inflammation — non-negotiable for combat-history athlete.
WHEY PROTEIN
08:30 AM · post-workout
Fastest leucine delivery to muscle. Triggers MPS within 30 minutes. The single most ROI-positive supplement in the stack.
CREATINE MONOHYDRATE
5g/DAY · any time · consistent
NEW ADDITION. 5g daily, any time of day — what matters is daily consistency, not timing. Most-researched supplement in sports nutrition. Boosts strength output, intramuscular water retention (visual fullness), and recovery. Vegetarians benefit most — your dietary creatine intake from non-meat sources is near zero. Mix with whey shake or any beverage. No loading phase needed — start at 5g/day from day one. Saturation occurs in 3-4 weeks.
ZMA
30-45 MIN PRE-SLEEP · empty stomach
CRITICAL: Away from dairy. Calcium blocks zinc absorption — separate from your 10:30 PM milk by at least 60 minutes. Take ZMA at 9:30 PM, then casein meal at 10:30 PM.
CHAPTER 06

RECOVERY FOR THE 30+ ATHLETE

CNS management for the combat veteran.

01
SLEEP ARCHITECTURE
8 hours minimum, consistent windows (±30 min). Growth hormone pulses during deep sleep are your primary recovery driver after 30. Cool room (18-20°C), zero screens 60 min before bed.
02
JOINT PREHAB
10 minutes daily. Band pull-aparts, face pulls, wrist circles, cat-cow, hip 90/90. Combat history = rotator cuffs, elbows, lumbar spine have miles on them. Insurance, not optional.
03
HRV MONITORING
If HRV drops 10%+ below 7-day rolling average, downshift that day's RPE by one full point. Not weakness — the difference between a 5-year program and burning out in 6 months.
04
DELOAD EVERY 5TH WEEK
Reduce working sets 40%, drop RPE to 6-7. You grow during recovery, not during the session. Combat athletes resist this — don't be one of them.
05
CONDITIONING CAP
Max 2x/week, 20-25 min steady-state OR 1 interval session. Heavy bag work over running for the combat itch — low impact, technique-focused, 3 rounds.
06
PARASYMPATHETIC RESET
5 min box breathing (4-4-4-4) post-training. Shifts you parasympathetic, accelerates recovery initiation. Cortisol is the enemy in a lean bulk. Sounds soft — it's not.

Train hard. Recover harder. The athlete who lasts is the one who learns to manage recovery as aggressively as he manages effort.
