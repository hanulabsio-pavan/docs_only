RV32IMAC, defended

Seven lessons. One purpose. You wrote RV32IMAC on your resume because of Hanu 1.0. An interviewer will now probe it. This track gives you the microarchitecture behind each letter, the hazards each one creates, and the traps panels actually set.

Ground rules for this track

  • Basic digital design is assumed. No revision of muxes, FSMs or setup-hold.
  • RV32 is the anchor. RV64 differences appear as marked delta notes.
  • Every lesson closes with Defend your claim prompts. You supply the Hanu 1.0 answers.
  • Reference microarchitecture is a single-issue in-order pipeline, unless stated otherwise.

1Why this track exists

A resume line is a contract. RV32IMAC promises you understand four orthogonal things. Each letter changes a different part of the pipeline.

crux

I sets the decode shape. M breaks fixed latency. A breaks speculation and memory ordering. C breaks instruction alignment. Four letters, four distinct microarchitectural problems.

Interviewers rarely ask "what is RV32I". They ask what breaks. The strong candidate answers in terms of the pipeline, not the mnemonic list.

RV32I is the base. M, A and C are standard extensions layered on it. The base is frozen. Extensions are ratified independently.

say it correctly

Do not call RV32IMAC a profile. It is an ISA string. RVA and RVB profiles are separate, later constructs targeted at application-class hardware. RV32IMAC is closer in intent to what an embedded or control-plane core ships.

context

Your portal targets RV64GC for the Hanuma SoC. G expands to IMAFD_Zicsr_Zifencei. So RV64GC is a strict superset of what this track covers, minus the 32-bit width. Everything here transfers.

5Primary sources

Quote the document, not a blog. Panels respect specification literacy.

  • RISC-V Unprivileged ISA specification — the volume that defines RV32I, M, A and C. Chapters on Zmmul, Zalrsc and Zaamo matter for the modern decomposition.
  • RISC-V Privileged specification — trap causes, mstatus, mtval, misaligned-access behaviour.
  • RVWMO appendix — preserved program order rules. This is where the aq/rl semantics are formally defined.
  • Zc* specification — the modern re-cut of C into Zca, Zcb, Zcf, Zcd, Zcmp, Zcmt.
Spec versions move. Before an interview, confirm the current ratified version numbers on riscv.org rather than quoting a number from memory.

6How to revise this track

  1. Read L01 to L04 once, in order. Do not skip the tabs.
  2. Answer every Defend your claim prompt in writing, using real Hanu 1.0 decisions.
  3. Read L05 and L06. These are where most candidates get caught.
  4. Drill L07 cold, out loud, without reading the answer first.
  5. Return to any lesson whose self-check you failed.
the meta-trap

Never claim more than Hanu 1.0 actually implemented. If you built I and M but stubbed A, say so plainly and describe what you designed for A. Panels forgive scope. They do not forgive bluffing.
