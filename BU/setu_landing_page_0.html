The product

One panel replaces the switchboard —
and elevates the room.

Setu installs in place of a standard Indian 3-gang or 4-gang switchboard. A tap, a gesture, or a spoken word controls your lighting and sets the mood of the entire room.

4

Touch controls

Four glass-smooth capacitive zones with instant, local response — plus a dedicated scene zone for one-touch ambiance.

Living themes

Devotional, Party, Movie, Sleep and Morning — curated scenes that set every connected light with one word or touch.

Smart features

Say the word.
The room follows.

Five curated themes transform your lighting in a single touch or phrase. Voice recognition runs entirely on the panel — no microphone stream ever leaves your home.

“Calm, warm light for prayer and quiet morning rituals.”

On-device voice wake-word · themes update over the air, so new scenes arrive without new hardware

Designed for reliability

A switch has one job:
to work, every single time.

Works without the internet

Every essential function executes locally inside the panel. If broadband fails, your switches don't.

Updates that can't brick it

New features arrive over the air. If an update ever fails, Setu automatically rolls back to the last working version.

Calm in electrical chaos

Touch sensing is calibrated for the electrical noise of real Indian 230V switchboards, and industrial-grade relays carry your loads.

Remembers through outages

After a power cut, Setu restores exactly what was on before — gently, without an inrush surge or a 3 a.m. surprise.

Safety engineered in

Designed to the Indian electrical safety standard IS 1293, with high-voltage isolation barriers and flame-retardant materials. BIS and WPC approvals are in progress.

Private by architecture

Voice is processed on-device; no audio is uploaded anywhere. Usage diagnostics are collected only with your explicit consent.
