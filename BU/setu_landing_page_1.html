Your home
doesn't care.

Every essential function runs inside the panel. The afternoon the WiFi dies is the afternoon you forget Setu is a smart product at all.

05.1

Fully offline by design

Touch, themes and voice execute locally — no cloud round-trip, ever, for daily control.

05.2

Power cut? It remembers.

After an outage, Setu restores exactly what was on — gently staggered, no inrush surge, no 3 a.m. all-on surprise.

05.3

Updates that can't brick it

New features arrive over the air. A failed update rolls back automatically to the last working version.

05.4
