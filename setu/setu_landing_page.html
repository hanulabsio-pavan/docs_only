05 · 13:30 · Under the glass, in plain words

Technology you
never have to think about.

05.1

The wall always wins

If a phone command and a hand at the wall ever disagree, the person in the room wins. A touch is never overridden by a late instruction from outside.

05.2

It quietly heals itself

A built-in watchdog monitors the panel continuously. If anything ever misbehaves, it recovers in moments — your lights stay exactly as they were.

05.3

Mains kept at arm's length

The 230V side lives behind engineered isolation barriers, physically separated from everything you touch. Designed to IS 1293, India's electrical safety standard.

05.4

Even if touch fails, light doesn't

The touch surface and the switching circuits are independent. In the unlikely event touch ever faults, whatever is on stays on — and other controls keep working.

15:04The broadband just died. Nobody in the house noticed.

06 · 15:00 · The internet goes down

Your home
doesn't care.

06.1

Fully offline by design

Touch, themes and voice execute inside the panel — no cloud round-trip, ever, for daily control. Broadband down means nothing changes at the wall.

06.2

Power cut? It remembers.

When electricity returns, Setu restores exactly what was on before — switched back gently, one load at a time, so there's no surge and no 3 a.m. all-on surprise.

06.3

Updates that can't brick it

New features install in the background in a separate memory bank. If an update ever fails, the panel boots straight back into the version that worked.

06.4

Built for Indian WiFi

Congested networks, frequent drops — the panel reconnects on its own and never makes your lights wait for the router.

IST · LOCALSchedules run inside the panel on Indian time — not on a server abroad.

07 · 17:30 · Dusk settles in

It knows
your evenings.

07.1

Schedules that know sunset

Set the balcony light to come on at dusk — the panel tracks sunset itself, locally, on Indian time. The schedule fires even if broadband is down that evening.

07.2

Every switch knows its name

“Balcony”, “Fan”, “Tube light” — name each switch during setup, so voice and app always speak your language, not circuit numbers.

07.3

Kid-proof. Clean-proof.

Child-lock makes any switch ignore curious fingers. Cleaning mode lets you wipe the glass without a light show. And rapid-fire tapping never stresses the relays — the panel paces itself.

07.4

Set up in minutes

First-time setup happens over Bluetooth from your phone — no hub to buy, no rewiring, no cloud account required to switch a light.
